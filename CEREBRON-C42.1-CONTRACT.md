## F72 REALITY REVIEW — MANDATORY

Before canonical promotion, actively search for errors rather than approval. The review must explicitly separate established facts from hypotheses and report, where applicable:

- ESTABLISHED: claims directly supported by current evidence.
- HYPOTHETICAL: assumptions, extrapolations and unverified claims.
- EVIDENCE_LEVEL: strength and independence of the supporting evidence.
- REAL_STATE: observed execution/repository/world state, distinct from intended state.
- PROOFS: reproducible artifacts, hashes, runs, measurements or formal derivations.
- COUNTEREVIDENCE: evidence that weakens or contradicts the claim.
- FRAGILE_ASSUMPTIONS: assumptions whose failure would change the conclusion.
- HIDDEN_DEPENDENCIES: shared data, models, tools, sources or infrastructure that can create false independence.
- CONTRADICTIONS: unresolved conflicts between claims, evidence or observed state.
- FALSE_CONSENSUS_RISK: apparent agreement caused by common dependencies, duplicated evidence or common prompting.
- WEAKNESSES: known gaps and untested boundaries.
- MAIN_RISK: the single most decision-relevant unresolved risk.
- NEXT_PRIORITY_ACTION: the next decisive verification, reproduction, ablation or transfer test.

A contradictory reproducible observation outranks approval or majority agreement. Missing evidence must lower the claim ceiling; it must not be replaced by confidence. Any unresolved material contradiction keeps the reality gate FAIL/HOLD.

Deterministic tools and adapters must not impersonate independent AI agents. Canonical promotion requires validation/reality gate.
